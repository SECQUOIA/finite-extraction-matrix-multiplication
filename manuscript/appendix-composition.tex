\section{The full composed relation maps}
\label{sec:composed-maps}

This appendix specifies the composition in
\eqref{eq:complete-relation-map} on its full variable spaces. In
particular, the sector-specific reindexing is a fixed linear map even
on positive-degree terms, whose first-leg label need not agree with the
other sector labels.

For a split action \(i\in\mathcal I_c\), write its row, source
column, and width as \(r_i,b_i,h_i\), and put \(d_i=h_i+1\).
Let \(w_i\) be its source count and \(n_{i,q}\) its child counts,
ordered by decreasing length \(b_i+h_i-2q\), \(0\le q\le h_i\).
Thus \(\sum_qn_{i,q}=w_i\). For a tripling relation in row \(r_i\),
use \(w_i=z_{r_i}\) in the formulas below. At type scale \(N\), put
\(\nu_i=Nw_i\); in the tripling case also put
\(m_i=\lfloor(\nu_i+1)/3\rfloor\). The source and canonical sector
tensors are
\[
 \mathcal U_i=
 \begin{cases}
 (C(r_i,b_i)B_X(d_i))^{\otimes \nu_i},&i\in\mathcal I_c,\\
 C(r_i,4r_i-1)^{\otimes \nu_i},&i\in\mathcal I_t,
 \end{cases}
\]
and
\[
 \mathcal V_i=
 \begin{cases}
 \displaystyle\prod_{q=0}^{h_i}
 C(r_i,b_i+h_i-2q)^{\otimes Nn_{i,q}},&i\in\mathcal I_c,\\
 C(r_i,r_i)^{\otimes(\nu_i-m_i)}
 (C(r_i,r_i)^{(23)})^{\otimes m_i},&i\in\mathcal I_t.
 \end{cases}
\]
The tripling sector is kept in this mixed orientation until the three
permutations are combined.

Let \(\mathsf T_i\) be the exact type map of
Lemma~\ref{lem:multiway} or Lemma~\ref{lem:tripling}. Enumerate its
retained words lexicographically by \(s=0,\ldots,M_i-1\), and write
\[
 \mathsf T_i(\mathcal U_i)=
 \sum_{s=0}^{M_i-1}\sum_{\alpha,\beta,\gamma}
 c_{i,s}(\alpha,\beta,\gamma)x_\alpha y_{s,\beta}z_{s,\gamma}.
\]
The first variable space \(X_i\) is shared. The other two spaces are
\(\bigoplus_sY_{i,s}\) and \(\bigoplus_sZ_{i,s}\), and the indices
\(\beta,\gamma\) range over the coordinates of sector \(s\).

Set \(L_i=2M_i-1\), and let \(\mathsf S_i(t)\) be the substitutions
of Proposition~\ref{prop:separator} with \(K_0=M_i,c=1\).
Applied after the type maps in the \(L_i\) source copies, they give
exactly
\begin{align}
 &\mathsf S_i(t)
   \left(\bigoplus_{\ell=0}^{L_i-1}\mathsf T_i\right)
   (L_i\mathcal U_i) \notag\\
 &\quad=
 \sum_{\substack{0\le s,g,u,v<M_i\\u-v+g-s=0}}
 t^{(g-s)^2}
 \sum_{\alpha,\beta,\gamma}
 c_{i,s}(\alpha,\beta,\gamma)
 X_{\alpha,g}Y_{s,\beta,u}Z_{s,\gamma,v}.
 \label{eq:composed-relation-polynomial}
\end{align}
This formula includes every positive-degree term. The Fourier sum
imposes the equality \(u-v+g-s=0\), rather than merely a congruence,
and the combined weight is \((g-s)^2\).

Here the coefficients are explicit integer arrays. For a split action,
let \(\omega_i(s)\in\{0,\ldots,h_i\}^{\nu_i}\) be its retained word.
In the determinant bases, before reordering positions,
\[
 c_{i,s}(\boldsymbol\alpha,\boldsymbol\beta,\boldsymbol\gamma)
 =\prod_{\theta=1}^{\nu_i}
   \mathbf1_{\{\gamma_\theta=\alpha_\theta+\beta_\theta\}}.
\]
At a position with label \(q=\omega_i(s)_\theta\), the coordinate ranges
are
\[
 0\le\alpha_\theta<r_i,\quad
 0\le\beta_\theta<b_i+h_i-2q,\quad
 0\le\gamma_\theta<r_i+b_i+h_i-2q-1.
\]
Thus the coefficient of
\(X_{\boldsymbol\alpha,g}Y_{s,\boldsymbol\beta,u}
Z_{s,\boldsymbol\gamma,v}\) in
\eqref{eq:composed-relation-polynomial} is
\[
 \mathbf1_{\{u-v+g-s=0\}}\,t^{(g-s)^2}
 \prod_{\theta=1}^{\nu_i}
   \mathbf1_{\{\gamma_\theta=\alpha_\theta+\beta_\theta\}}.
\]
For a tripling relation, the same product of indicators applies when
\(\beta_\theta,\gamma_\theta\) are the original interval coordinates
from Lemma~\ref{lem:tripling}. The word-specific shifts and first-index
reversals are part of the subsequent map, not this coefficient array.

The separator target spaces are
\[
 \widetilde X_i=\bigoplus_gX_i^{(g)},\qquad
 \widetilde Y_i=\bigoplus_s(Y_{i,s}\otimes\C^{M_i}),\qquad
 \widetilde Z_i=\bigoplus_s(Z_{i,s}\otimes\C^{M_i}).
\]
For each word \(s\), let
\((\mathsf R^X_{i,s},\mathsf R^Y_{i,s},\mathsf R^Z_{i,s})\)
be the coordinate permutations identifying its sector tensor with
\(\mathcal V_i\). For a split, they order positions by increasing
filtration index, preserving the original order within each class. For
tripling, they place all outer positions before all middle positions,
again preserving their order; they shift the interval coordinates and
reverse the first index at middle positions. Both outer labels have the
same canonical block dimensions, so \(\mathcal V_i\) is independent
of the numbers of left and right labels.

Define the reindexing on the full target spaces by
\[
 \mathsf R_i=
 \left(
  \bigoplus_g \mathsf R^X_{i,g},\quad
  \bigoplus_s(\mathsf R^Y_{i,s}\otimes I_{M_i}),\quad
  \bigoplus_s(\mathsf R^Z_{i,s}\otimes I_{M_i})
 \right).
\]
The first component uses the word indexed by \(g\), even in a
monomial whose other sector label is \(s\ne g\). This defines a
coordinate permutation on every target variable. Its behavior on those
positive-degree monomials need not identify them with sector tensors;
only the constant term has that form. All three maps are independent
of \(t\).

Choose the row sign \(\sigma_i=(-1)^{a-r_i}\), and let
\(\pi\in\mathcal A_{\sigma_i}\). The complete map
\(\Psi_{i,\pi}(t)\) acts on \(L_i\mathcal U_i^\pi\); its separator
uses the global leg occupied by the native first leg after permutation
by \(\pi\). The constant term in
\eqref{eq:composed-relation-polynomial} has \(g=s\) and \(u=v\), so
\[
 \Psi_{i,\pi}(t)(L_i\mathcal U_i^\pi)
 =\bigoplus_{s=0}^{M_i-1}(\mathcal V_iB_X(M_i))^\pi
   +\sum_{e=1}^{(M_i-1)^2}t^eG_{i,\pi,e}.
\]
The first term is a full direct sum on all three legs.

Now tensor over the three \(\pi\in\mathcal A_{\sigma_i}\). A
direct-sum block is indexed by a triple of independently chosen sector
labels. In every such block the canonical tensor is the same product
\(\bigotimes_\pi(\mathcal V_iB_X(M_i))^\pi\). Regrouping its factors
balances the dot products into \(\MM_{M_i}\). For tripling, apply
\eqref{eq:cyclic-tripling-balance} to its convolution factors. This is
a fixed coordinate isomorphism of the canonical product spaces, applied
separately on every block of each global leg. In particular it is also
defined on positive-degree monomials whose three block labels differ.
It introduces no degree or interpolation cost.

Finally tensor over all actions, using one parameter. The source and
constant-term multiplicities are
\[
 \prod_iL_i^3=S,\qquad \prod_iM_i^3=H,
\]
and Lemma~\ref{lem:composition} bounds the degree by
\(3\sum_i(M_i-1)^2=D\). The signed ledger identifies common source
and target convolution factors by the actual coordinate isomorphisms
\(S^\sigma_{r,b}\cong S^{-\sigma}_{b,r}\), then retains them as the
catalyst. Combining the auxiliary factors by
\eqref{eq:matrix-products} gives precisely
\eqref{eq:catalytic-degeneration}. All final regroupings are fixed
maps on the full spaces, not instructions defined only for the
constant term.
