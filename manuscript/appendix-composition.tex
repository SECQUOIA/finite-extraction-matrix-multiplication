\section{The full composed relation maps}
\label{sec:composed-maps}

This appendix writes the composition in
\eqref{eq:complete-relation-map} on its full variable spaces. In
particular, a sector-specific reindexing is a fixed linear map even on
the positive-degree terms, whose sector labels need not agree.

Fix the scale \(N\) of Section~\ref{sec:extraction}. For relation \(i\),
let its source tensor and its canonical sector tensor be
\[
 \mathcal U_i=
 \begin{cases}
  (C(r,b)B_X(2))^{\otimes Nw_i},&i\in\mathcal I_c,\\
  C(r,4r-1)^{\otimes Nz_i},&i\in\mathcal I_t,
 \end{cases}
\]
and
\[
 \mathcal V_i=
 \begin{cases}
  C(r,b+1)^{\otimes Nu_i}C(r,b-1)^{\otimes Nd_i},
       &i\in\mathcal I_c,\\
  C(r,r)^{\otimes 2Nz_i/3}(C(r,r)^\sigma)^{\otimes Nz_i/3},
       &i\in\mathcal I_t,
 \end{cases}
\]
where \(\sigma\) exchanges the second and third legs. In the tripling
case, the exchanged factors remain in this expression until the six
permutations are combined.

Let \(\mathsf T_i\) be the exact type map of
Lemma~\ref{lem:determinant} or Lemma~\ref{lem:tripling}. Enumerate its
retained words by \(h=0,\ldots,M_i-1\), and write
\[
 \mathsf T_i(\mathcal U_i)=
 \sum_{h=0}^{M_i-1}\sum_{\alpha,\beta,\gamma}
 c_{i,h}(\alpha,\beta,\gamma)x_\alpha y_{h,\beta}z_{h,\gamma}.
\]
The first variable space \(X_i\) is shared. The other two spaces are
\(\bigoplus_hY_{i,h}\) and \(\bigoplus_hZ_{i,h}\), and the indices
\(\beta,\gamma\) range over the coordinates of sector \(h\).

Set \(L_i=2M_i-1\), and let \(\mathsf S_i(t)\) be the substitutions
of Proposition~\ref{prop:separator} with \(K_0=M_i,c=1\).
After the type maps in the \(L_i\) source copies, these give exactly
\begin{align}
 &\mathsf S_i(t)
   \left(\bigoplus_{\ell=0}^{L_i-1}\mathsf T_i\right)
   (L_i\mathcal U_i) \notag\\
 &\quad=
 \sum_{\substack{0\le h,g,u,v<M_i\\u-v+g-h=0}}
 t^{(g-h)^2}
 \sum_{\alpha,\beta,\gamma}
 c_{i,h}(\alpha,\beta,\gamma)
 X_{\alpha,g}Y_{h,\beta,u}Z_{h,\gamma,v}.
 \label{eq:composed-relation-polynomial}
\end{align}
The Fourier sum imposes the displayed equality, and the combined weight
is \((g-h)^2\). This formula includes every positive-degree term.

For example, for a neighboring-length relation put \(n=Nw_i\), and
let \(\omega(h)\in\{+,-\}^n\) be its retained word. In the quotient
and kernel bases, before reordering positions,
\[
 c_{i,h}(\boldsymbol\alpha,\boldsymbol\beta,\boldsymbol\gamma)
 =\prod_{\nu=1}^n\mathbf1_{\{\gamma_\nu=\alpha_\nu+\beta_\nu\}}.
\]
Here \(0\le\alpha_\nu<r\). At a \(+\) position,
\(0\le\beta_\nu\le b\) and \(0\le\gamma_\nu\le r+b-1\);
at a \(-\) position, \(0\le\beta_\nu\le b-2\) and
\(0\le\gamma_\nu\le r+b-3\).
Thus the coefficient of
\(X_{\boldsymbol\alpha,g}Y_{h,\boldsymbol\beta,u}
Z_{h,\boldsymbol\gamma,v}\) in
\eqref{eq:composed-relation-polynomial} is
\[
 \mathbf1_{\{u-v+g-h=0\}}\,t^{(g-h)^2}
 \prod_{\nu=1}^n\mathbf1_{\{\gamma_\nu=\alpha_\nu+\beta_\nu\}}.
\]
For a tripling relation the coefficient is likewise a product of
\(\mathbf1_{\{\gamma_\nu=\alpha_\nu+\beta_\nu\}}\), with the original interval
coordinates in Lemma~\ref{lem:tripling}; the middle-position reversals
are part of the subsequent reindexing.

The separator target spaces are
\[
 \widetilde X_i=\bigoplus_gX_i^{(g)},\qquad
 \widetilde Y_i=\bigoplus_h(Y_{i,h}\otimes\C^{M_i}),\qquad
 \widetilde Z_i=\bigoplus_h(Z_{i,h}\otimes\C^{M_i}).
\]
For each \(h\), let
\((\mathsf R^X_{i,h},\mathsf R^Y_{i,h},\mathsf R^Z_{i,h})\) be the coordinate
permutations identifying its sector tensor with \(\mathcal V_i\).
They group tensor positions by type; in the tripling case they also
shift each second- and third-leg interval to start at zero and reverse
the first index at middle positions. Define the maps on the full
target spaces by
\[
 \mathsf R_i=
 \left(
  \bigoplus_h \mathsf R^X_{i,h},\quad
  \bigoplus_h(\mathsf R^Y_{i,h}\otimes I_{M_i}),\quad
  \bigoplus_h(\mathsf R^Z_{i,h}\otimes I_{M_i})
 \right).
\]
The first component acts on block \(h\) of \(\widetilde X_i\), whether
or not a monomial's other sector labels equal \(h\). Hence all three
maps are defined on every term of
\eqref{eq:composed-relation-polynomial}, and they are independent of \(t\).

The map \(\Phi_{i,\pi}(t)\) of \eqref{eq:complete-relation-map}
acts on \(L_i\mathcal U_i^\pi\). Its separator acts along the leg
occupied by \(X_i\) after permutation by \(\pi\).
The constant term in \eqref{eq:composed-relation-polynomial} has
\(g=h\) and \(u=v\), so
\[
 \Phi_{i,\pi}(t)(L_i\mathcal U_i^\pi)
 =\bigoplus_{h=0}^{M_i-1}(\mathcal V_iB_X(M_i))^\pi
   +\sum_{d=1}^{(M_i-1)^2}t^dG_{i,\pi,d}.
\]
In particular, the constant term has \(M_i\) full direct-sum copies.

Tensor \(\Phi_{i,\pi}(t)\) over all \(i,\pi\), using one parameter.
The source and constant-term multiplicities are
\(\prod_iL_i^6=S\) and \(\prod_iM_i^6=H\).
Lemma~\ref{lem:composition} bounds the degree by
\(6\sum_i(M_i-1)^2=D\). Regroup the source factors and the
constant-term factors by coordinate permutations independent of \(t\).
For tripling, the mixed factors \(\mathcal V_i^\pi\) combine over
all six permutations to \(Q_{r,r}^{\otimes Nz_i}\), by the
orientation-balance identity in Section~\ref{sec:maps}.
The ledger \eqref{eq:formal-ledger} and the dot-product identities
\eqref{eq:matrix-products} then give exactly
\eqref{eq:catalytic-degeneration}.
