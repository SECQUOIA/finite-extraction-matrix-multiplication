\subsection{An explicit rate of convergence}
\label{subsec:quantitative}

The preceding estimates give more than convergence to \(9/4\).
The following bounds specify an index for every exponent allowance.
The constants are deliberately conservative.

\begin{theorem}[A fully specified family]\label{thm:quantitative}
For an integer \(a\ge2^{64}\), choose
\[
 \ell_a=\lceil\log_2a\rceil,\qquad
 K=a^3\ell_a,\qquad N=k=1,\qquad j=a\ell_a.
\]
Apply conserved rounding to the maximal-width occupations, and use
the resulting integer types in the finite construction.
The relative scheme has \(B>A\). Its pooled recursive exponent \(p\)
satisfies
\begin{equation}\label{eq:explicit-rate}
 p<\frac94+\frac{900}{\log a},
 \qquad
 \log A\le181a^5\ell_a^2.
\end{equation}
In particular, the complete finite construction has exponent tending
to \(9/4\) and \(\log A=O(a^5(\log a)^2)\).
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Write \(t=\log a\) and \(\ell=\ell_a\). Then \(t>32\),
\(\ell\ge64\), \(t/\log2\le\ell\le a\), and \(\log K\le4t\).
We first bound the contraction, including both rounding and type losses.
The candidate coordinate count and the number of active relations obey
\(J_a\le2a^3\) and \(I\le2a^2\).
The occupation and rounding bounds give, for each action and reset,
\[
 w_i\le180K+2a,\qquad z_r\le2K+1.
\]
Consequently the type loss at \(N=1\) is bounded by
\begin{equation}\label{eq:quantitative-type-loss}
 \mathcal T_1\le
 J_a\log(180K+2a+1)+(a-1)\log(2K+2).
\end{equation}
Here the sum of the action support sizes minus one is at most \(J_a\),
and there are at most \(a-1\) positive resets.
Since \(180K+2a+1\le181K\), \(\log181<6\), and \(\log4<2\),
\[
 \frac{4J_a+\mathcal T_1}{K}
 \le\frac{20+8t+(4t+2)/a^2}{\ell}
 \le\frac{\frac{41}{2}+9t}{\ell}
 <\frac{41}{128}+\frac{63}{10}.
\]
The first two inequalities use \((a-1)/a^3\le1/a^2\le1/4\);
the last uses \(\ell\ge64\) and \(t/\ell\le\log2<7/10\).

There is also a convenient elementary lower bound for the profile.
The inequality \(\log(1+x)\ge x-x^2/2\), for \(x\ge0\), gives
\[
 \log s_a
 \ge\frac13\sum_{h=1}^{a-1}\frac1h
     -\frac1{18}\sum_{h=1}^{a-1}\frac1{h^2}
 \ge\frac13\log a-\frac19.
\]
As \(F(a,a)=s_a(3a-1)/2\ge a s_a\), we obtain
\(\log F(a,a)\ge(4/3)t-1/9\).
The conserved potential identity and the type bound therefore imply
\begin{equation}\label{eq:quantitative-contraction}
 \frac{\log(v/u)}{K}
 \ge\log F(a,a)-\frac{4J_a+\mathcal T_1}{K}
 >\frac43t-7>0,
\end{equation}
where \(1/9+41/128+63/10<7\). This proves \(B>A\).

We next bound every term in the exponent numerator.
The copy charge satisfies
\[
 \frac{\log(S/H)}{K}
 \le\frac{6}{a\ell}\le1.
\]
For interpolation, each action entropy is at most its auxiliary charge,
and the per-row occupation estimate gives
\[
 H_i\le w_i\log d_i\le180K+2a\log(2a).
\]
The reset entropy \(z_r\log3\) is smaller than the same upper bound.
Thus, writing \(B_1\) for the largest of these entropy bounds,
\[
 \frac{\log E}{K}
 \le\frac{\log(3I+1)+2B_1}{K}
 \le360+\frac{5}{a\ell}\le361.
\]
In the middle inequality we used
\(\log(6a^2+1)\le2a\) and \(\log(2a)\le a\), valid for \(a\ge2\).

Finally, the rounded count bounds imply
\[
 U+T-K\le182aK+2a^3+a<184aK.
\]
All catalyst rank bases are less than \(5a\), so
\[
 \frac{\log\kappa_1}{jK}
 \le552\frac{\log(5a)}{\ell}
 <552\cdot\frac7{10}\left(1+\frac28\right)=483.
\]
This uses \(\log2<7/10\), \(\log5<2\), and \(t>8\).
Together with \(3\log(2a-1)<3t+3\), these estimates bound the
pooled exponent numerator by \(K(3t+850)\).
This bound even allows the additional fixed-batch ceiling charge
\(\log2/j\), which is less than \(K\).
Combining it with \eqref{eq:quantitative-contraction} gives
\[
 p<\frac{3t+850}{\frac43t-7}
 =\frac94+\frac{850+63/4}{\frac43t-7}
 <\frac94+\frac{900}{t},
\]
because \((4/3)t-7\ge t\) for \(t>32\).
Conversely, every overhead is nonnegative and
\(\log(v/u)\le K\log F(a,a)\), so
\[
 p\ge\frac{3\log(2a-1)}{\log F(a,a)}>\frac94
\]
by the strict diagonal bound in Theorem~\ref{thm:least-profile}.
The upper and lower bounds prove the claimed limit.

For the source dimension, use \(C_a\le180a\) and the rounded
auxiliary bound to obtain
\[
 \log A=j\log u
 \le a\ell\bigl(180aK+2a^3\log(2a)\bigr)
 =a^5\ell^2\left(180+
                 \frac{2\log(2a)}{a\ell}\right)
 \le181a^5\ell^2.
\]
The last inequality follows from \(\log(2a)\le a\) and \(\ell\ge64\).
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[An explicit index for an exponent allowance]
\label{cor:explicit-allowance}
Given a positive rational \(\eps\), put
\[
 \ell=\max\left\{64,\left\lceil\frac{1800}{\eps}\right\rceil\right\},
 \qquad a=2^\ell.
\]
The family in Theorem~\ref{thm:quantitative} gives a finite relative
scheme with recursive exponent strictly below \(9/4+\eps\), and
\[
 \log A\le181\,2^{5\ell}\ell^2.
\]
The index and all four construction parameters are selected by integer
and rational computations.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
Here \(\ell_a=\ell\) and \(\log a=\ell\log2>\ell/2\). Therefore
\(900/\log a<1800/\ell\le\eps\), and the source bound follows
from \eqref{eq:explicit-rate}.
\end{proof}

The bound concerns the recursive source dimension \(A\) of the relative
scheme. It does not bound the side of a rational square seed obtained
after coefficient descent, or the time and bit complexity needed to
write out all coefficients of the construction.
