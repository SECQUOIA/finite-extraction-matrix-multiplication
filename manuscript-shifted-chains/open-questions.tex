\section{Open questions}
\label{sec:open}

\begin{enumerate}
\item \emph{Smaller finite sizes.} For the chain schemes of
Definition~\ref{def:class} with \(p\le9/4+\eps\), the least copy counts
have logarithms of order \(\eps^{-9/2}\log^3(1/\eps)\)
(Remark~\ref{rem:limits-orders}), but the constants of the two bounds
are far apart. Do compressed auxiliaries
(Definition~\ref{def:compressed}) that attain~\eqref{eq:hankel-bound}
exist at large widths, and what sizes do they give? What is the least
source side over all supplies? Can an evaluation of the source with
fewer than \(s^{6W}\) products (Section~\ref{subsec:limits-scope})
reduce the sizes?

\item \emph{Coupled auxiliaries.} The limits of
Section~\ref{sec:limits} assume an independent auxiliary for every block,
stage and orientation, and separate exactification of every stage. Can
auxiliaries shared between orientations or stages, or joint
exactification of several stages, change the orders of
Theorem~\ref{thm:lower}?

\item \emph{Seeds without the factor \(1/\eps\).} The seed of
Theorem~\ref{thm:seed} has \(\log n_0\ge(24/\eps)\log A\), and for
\(\eps\le3/4\) the unrolling procedure needs a factor of this order
(Remark~\ref{rem:seed-factor}). Is there a rational seed with exponent
below \(9/4+\eps\) and \(\log n_0=O(\eps^{-9/2}\log^3(1/\eps))\)? It
would improve the rate of the seed route of
Remark~\ref{rem:seed-route}. Independently, can the rate \(2/11\) of
Theorem~\ref{thm:uniform} be improved?

\item \emph{Bit complexity and precision.} Block recursion with a fixed
rational seed is covered by~\citep[Lemma~9.4 and
Propositions~9.5--9.6]{FinCon26}, but Remark~\ref{rem:precision} bounds
the precision of one application of the scheme only. What are the
precision and the integer bit complexity of the pooled recursion, which
Algorithm~\(\mathcal U\) of Section~\ref{sec:seeds-uniform} uses?

\item \emph{Positive characteristic.} The local maps of
Section~\ref{sec:stages} hold over every field. The separator and
exactification are charged here by rational interpolation, and we prove
our results in characteristic zero only; the common denominator
\(\mathcal D\) of Proposition~\ref{prop:coefficients} is divisible by
\((s-1)!\). Over every field, \(\omega\le9/4\) is
known~\citep{Nav26a,Zui26,Fil26}, but without sizes. Is there a
construction over every field, for example over \(\mathbb F_2\), whose
copy counts have logarithms polynomial in \(1/\eps\)?

\item \emph{Practical sizes.} Every chain scheme with \(p<3\) has more
than \(2^{494}\) target copies (Proposition~\ref{prop:small-sources}),
and far from \(9/4\) other constructions are much smaller
(Section~\ref{subsec:examples-comparison}). Which exponents can shifted
stages, alone or combined with other schemes, reach with sizes that can
be executed in practice?
\end{enumerate}
