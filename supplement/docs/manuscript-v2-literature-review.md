# Focused literature review for the revised construction

Review cutoff: 7 October 2026. This review covers the methods added or materially revised in the v2 construction: multiway determinant filtrations, threefold tensor balancing, integral circulation rounding, entropy loss, harmonic occupation flows, and pooled relative recursion. It supplements [the earlier manuscript literature review](manuscript-literature-review.md), which treats the 9/4 claim, the closest OpenAI preprint comparison, the scalar profile certificate, and known exponent bounds. This pass did not rerun experiments or edit manuscript TeX.

## Assessment

The v2 construction combines established tools with a more specific finite tensor application. The integral Clebsch–Gordan filtration, cyclic permutation of tensor legs, circulation integrality, flow rounding, fixed-total type approximation, absorbing-chain occupation formulas, Doob transforms, and Schönhage-type simultaneous-product comparisons all have clear antecedents. The focused search did not find a source that gives the paper’s compatible convolution maps, exact sector restrictions, signed resource ledger, and finite cost analysis as one construction. That is a bounded comparison, not a global priority claim.

The statement about neighboring lengths must preserve an important qualification: the restriction follows **after the explicit basis change** in the determinant-filtration coordinates. It is not a coordinate restriction in the original monomial basis.

For citation practice, the manuscript can attribute the classical ingredients to the sources above and state that its new contribution is their concrete convolution-profile application and complete accounting. Use Stothers for theorem-level finite sum-inequality details unless the original Schönhage or Strassen scan is independently made readable. Do not describe the generalized circulation theorem, the Doob transform, the Clebsch–Gordan filtration, or cyclic symmetrization as new.

## Novelty scope and consistency check

The most defensible v2 novelty statement is limited to the precise construction: (i) explicit basis coordinates make the determinant filtration compatible with convolution multiplication; (ii) fixed total filtration counts produce exact neighboring-length sectors; (iii) a signed threefold ledger, where applicable, keeps the catalyst and resource counts exact; (iv) rational occupation flows can be rounded at any positive integer scale without clearing their denominators; and (v) the paper proves the entropy loss and finite recursion costs for these specific maps. The partial-middle tripling count
`M(t,h)=binom(t,h) 2^(t-h)`, with `h=floor((t+1)/3)` and `M(t,h) ≥ 3^t/(t+1)`, is likewise a specific exact sector restriction. These are application-level claims supported by the manuscript’s proofs, not claims that their general ingredients are new.

The v2 sources disclose no contradiction with known tensor lower bounds. The manuscript proves an upper bound and does not claim optimality; quadratic rank or border-rank lower bounds give an exponent floor of 2 and do not rule out 9/4. This methods-only pass did not repeat the broader lower-bound audit; see the earlier review’s “Status and possible conflicts” section for its source-level discussion. Nor does a finite family of upper-bound algorithms assert that the asymptotic-rank value equals its limit.

The two entropy statements now used in the manuscript are correctly separated. In the introduction, Böcherer–Geiger is cited for **optimal fixed-total probability approximations**. In the rounding section, Eq. (42) is said to bound the relative entropy of the minimizing M-type and to yield `3d` only for that optimum; the project’s feasible flow-rounded vector may be nonoptimal and may have a different total. Keep this wording. The basis-change qualification for the neighboring-length map should remain explicit. The v2 draft omits the cost-obstruction appendix; this review makes no recommendation to cite it.
